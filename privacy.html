OUT LOUD

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

The short version. Out Loud does not collect your personal information. Your camera is a mirror and nothing it sees is recorded or sent anywhere. Your voice is matched to words and never stored. Your affirmations, streak and photographs stay on your phone. There is no account and no sign-up.

Your camera

During a session the front camera shows your own face, live. It works the way a mirror works.

No video is recorded, saved, or transmitted. The preview exists only while the screen is open.

Daily frames

If daily frames are switched on, the app saves one still photograph per session, taken while you are speaking. These photographs:

  • are stored in private app storage on your device
  • are never uploaded, transmitted, or backed up by us
  • are not added to your camera roll unless you export one yourself
  • can be deleted all at once from within the app

Deleting the app removes them permanently.

Your voice

While you speak, the app uses your device's speech recognition to work out which words you have said, so the right words light up.

Audio is never recorded or stored by us, and never sent to our servers. Speech is processed on your device wherever your phone supports it. On devices or languages without offline recognition available, Apple's system speech service may process the audio. That is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we never receive it.

Silent mode never activates the microphone at all.

What stays on your device

WhatWhere

None of this is transmitted to us. We hold no copy and have no way to read it.

What connects to the internet

Three things reach the network. None of them carry information about you.

Backdrops

The backdrops shown when the camera is off are downloaded from our content server. The app requests the list of available backdrops and downloads the images you view. These requests contain no information about you — no name, no identifier, no affirmations. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs including IP address, as any web server does.

Typefaces

Typefaces are downloaded from Google Fonts the first time they are needed, then cached on your device. Google may log the request. See Google Fonts privacy.

Purchases

If you subscribe, payment is handled by Apple and the subscription is verified through RevenueCat, which manages subscription status for us.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and your purchase receipt. It does not receive your name, email, affirmations, photographs, or anything you do inside the app. We never see your payment details; Apple handles those. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved and we do not know whether you opened one.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we hold none
  • No account, email address, or phone number required
  • We do not know who you are

Children

Out Loud is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us — though in practice there is nothing for us to delete, as we hold nothing.

Deleting your data

Open the app, go to You, then Delete all my data. That erases your streak, history, saved frames, written affirmations and settings immediately and permanently.

Deleting the app has the same effect.

Your subscription is held by Apple, not by us. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings under your Apple ID.

This is not medical care

Out Loud is a self-guided practice app. It is not therapy, not medical treatment, and not a substitute for professional care. If you are struggling, please speak to a qualified professional.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app.
