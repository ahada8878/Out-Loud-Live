What Out Loud is

Out Loud is a self-guided app for practising spoken affirmations. It shows you a line, follows along while you say it aloud, and keeps a record of the days you practised.

It is not medical care. Out Loud is not therapy, not counselling, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional help. It does not diagnose or treat anything.
